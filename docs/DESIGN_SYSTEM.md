# UBA Design System

## Brand

**United Business Alliance (UBA)**  
**NATIONAL BUSINESS, TRADE & ECONOMIC ADVISORY COUNCIL**

Positioning line: **A National Business Platform for a Stronger Pakistan.**

Use the approved UBA emblem and wordmark supplied by the organisation. Do not redraw or improvise the logo when approved files are available.

## Palette

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Forest green | #14543A | Primary institutional colour |
| Deep green | #174F36 | Dark surfaces and strong headings |
| Brushed gold | #B88E36 | Accent, separators and restrained emphasis |
| Warm ivory | #F4F2EA | Editorial backgrounds |
| White | #FFFFFF | Content surfaces |
| Charcoal | #263238 | Body text |
| Muted grey | #667078 | Secondary text and metadata |

Use contrast-checked combinations. Gold is an accent and should not carry small body copy on ivory.

## Visual principles

- Restrained, institutional and editorial.
- Clear typographic hierarchy.
- Generous whitespace and consistent grid.
- Thin gold dividers used sparingly.
- Strong responsive behaviour.
- Subtle transitions only when they aid comprehension.
- Avoid visual clutter and generic corporate stock imagery.
- Never imitate a government seal or imply state recognition.
- Do not use FPCCI marks in a manner implying endorsement or ownership.

## Homepage composition

1. Header with UBA identity and concise navigation.
2. Hero with the institutional descriptor, mission and a single primary action.
3. About/mission introduction.
4. Leadership feature using approved profiles only.
5. Policy and advocacy areas.
6. National chapters.
7. FPCCI election information, clearly labelled as UBA content.
8. Latest news and press releases.
9. Membership call to action.
10. Institutional footer with verified contact details and legal links.

## Typography

Use a refined serif for selected institutional display headings and a highly legible sans-serif for navigation and body copy. Prefer licensed, web-safe or open-source fonts. Load only required weights and subsets.

## Components

Create reusable components for:
- Site header and footer
- Primary and secondary navigation
- Editorial hero
- Section headings
- Leadership cards
- Chapter directory
- Policy cards
- News/article cards
- Document download rows
- Membership form fields
- Status labels
- Admin tables and approval panels

## Responsive rules

Design mobile-first. Navigation must remain usable by keyboard and touch. Tables need responsive alternatives. Forms must provide clear labels, errors, and success states.

## Brand asset handling

Store approved logo files in a dedicated public asset directory with source and usage notes. Do not convert low-resolution previews into master logos. Maintain clear-space and minimum-size guidance when source artwork is supplied.
