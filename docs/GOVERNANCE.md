# UBA Governance and Publication Controls

This document defines technical safeguards for institutional accuracy. It does not substitute for the UBA constitution, registration documents or legal advice.

## Proposed and confirmed status

Public website content must distinguish:
- Proposed
- Submitted for approval
- Approved
- Effective
- Expired
- Revoked

A proposed office-bearer must never appear in the public leadership directory as confirmed.

## Authority and records

- The Founding Council or other competent authority must be determined by the adopted constitutional documents and valid resolutions.
- The CMS should store the relevant resolution reference and approval date for appointments.
- The Secretary General or delegated Secretariat officer may publish an appointment notice only after verifying the approval record.
- Changes to approved governance records must preserve history.

## Public statements

Press releases, spokesperson statements and official notices should have:
- Named originating office
- Draft/review/approval/published status
- Approval record or authorised delegation
- Publication timestamp
- Correction history

## FPCCI references

UBA may publish its own election-related information and campaign materials where permitted. The website must not:
- Imply that UBA is FPCCI.
- Use FPCCI branding in a way that suggests endorsement.
- Claim official electoral authority or candidate eligibility without verification.
- Publish unverified election dates, rules, endorsements or results.

## Partner and recognition claims

Publish a partner, affiliation, government recognition or statutory status claim only where documentary evidence and permission to publish are recorded.

## Separation from PBF

Do not imply that PBF and UBA are the same entity, affiliated entities, predecessor/successor entities or that either controls the other unless formally documented and approved. Do not copy PBF's private data or partner assets into UBA.
